# War-room art

Created with the built-in Imagegen tool for the October 2026 dark medieval UI direction. Generated originals remain untouched; these JPEG exports are served locally. The attached user references informed the art direction. The six portraits represent chess roles; variant names and abilities come from the real catalog.

- [Fortress banner](./war-room-fortress.jpg): A weathered medieval fortress, dark oak gate, nocturnal fog, restrained torchlight and flying embers. Steel-blue moonlight, obsidian shadows, bronze details. Painterly dark fantasy realism. Fortress toward upper right and dark space at lower left for the headline. No text, logos, frames or UI.
- [Role portrait atlas](./war-room-roles.jpg): Exactly three columns and two rows of isolated dark fantasy portraits. Top: iron-crowned king, crimson sorceress queen, living castle rook. Bottom: hooded staff-bearing bishop, armored mounted knight, shield-and-spear pawn. Dark smoke, iron, bronze, leather, stone pedestals and restrained candlelight. Equal cells, no letters, no UI or borders. Rendered as CSS background regions.

Fonts are self-hosted in [fonts](../fonts/), with their respective SIL Open Font License files.
